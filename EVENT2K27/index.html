About SVCN EventEase

SVCN EventEase is a college event management platform designed to make event discovery and participation simple and convenient for students.

Students can explore upcoming events, register for activities and stay updated with college programmes. Administrators can manage events and registrations through the platform.

Our goal is to create a connected and engaging campus experience.
